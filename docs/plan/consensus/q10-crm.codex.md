# Q10 CRM / Customer Management

**Decision.** Build CRM as an Opzava-owned, tenant-scoped Postgres bounded context. `Contact` is the person aggregate root: PII, lifecycle, owner, dedupe fingerprints, consent refs, and embedded `ChannelIdentity` value objects. `ChannelIdentity = {tenantId, channel, accountId?, kind=dm_user|email|phone|provider_user, externalSenderId, normalizedValue?, verifiedBy, directoryName?, linkedAt}`; it identifies a sender, not a room. `Account/Company` is the organization aggregate with hierarchy and account-owner links. `Deal/Opportunity` owns commercial state and references `Account`, primary `Contact`, and `Pipeline/Stage` reference data. `Activity` is an append-only CRM event row for messages, calls, notes, campaign touches, deal/ticket changes. `Ticket` is a support aggregate referencing `Contact`, external `ConversationRef`, SLA, status, and assigned support `AgentEmployee`. `Segment` stores a saved CRM query plus materialized members. `Consent` stores per-contact, per-channel, per-purpose opt-in/out proof.

**Channel identity resolution.** The ACL is the only reader of OpenClaw channels and directory. Canonical identity comes from inbound routing facts (`channel`, `accountId`, sender id, peer kind, thread/topic where relevant); OpenClaw `directory` is an enrichment/lookup path for names-to-IDs and IDs-to-names, not the CRM source of truth. Auto-link only when `(tenantId, channel, accountId, externalSenderId)` matches exactly one verified `ChannelIdentity`. Misses create an `UnresolvedContactLink` shell and suggestion list from normalized phone/email, directory display name, and tenant-scoped dedupe fingerprints. Cross-channel same-person dedupe is suggest-and-confirm; merges are manual audited commands. Never search or merge across tenants, and never bind a Contact to a group/channel room id; group conversations carry sender identity plus separate `ConversationRef`.

**Conversations and support.** External Channels remain distinct from Internal Collaboration. The broker projects ACL conversation reads into `ExternalConversationReadModel` and appends Contact `Activity` rows idempotently. Support intent, channel binding, or queue rule opens/updates one `Ticket` for `(tenantId, conversationRef, contactId?)`; assignment targets a support `AgentEmployee` whose OpenClaw binding is limited to approved customer channels and policy scope.

**Marketing.** `Segment` is a saved query/specification, materialized asynchronously for campaigns and rebuilt from CRM truth. Campaign sends must intersect segment membership with `Consent(channel, purpose)` at send time; webhooks for unsubscribe, bounce, spam, or manual opt-out revoke consent and suppress future sends per GDPR/CAN-SPAM.

**Erasure.** `EraseContact(tenantId, contactId)` is an admin/provisioning workflow with idempotency key and PII-free audit tombstone. It hard-deletes or anonymizes Opzava Contact PII, `ChannelIdentity`, consent, segment members, and activity bodies according to retention policy, then calls the per-tenant Gateway through the ACL/admin context to delete channel history for known sender IDs and purge memory-wiki/lancedb references. Derived projections and indexes are rebuildable, so rebuild after scrub rather than hand-editing caches.

**Biggest sad path + invariant.** Sad path: a spoofed or mis-resolved channel sender links a customer conversation to the wrong Contact, contaminating tickets, deals, AI memory, campaign targeting, and legal exports. Invariant: no external conversation/activity/ticket write may commit unless it carries `tenantId` and either exactly one verified `ChannelIdentity` or an unresolved shell; uniqueness is `(tenantId, channel, accountId, externalSenderId)`, and raw external IDs never bypass the resolver.

**Parity + lean ops.** OpenClaw owns channel routing, sessions, sender IDs, group policy, and transcripts; Opzava owns CRM truth and rebuildable projections. One broker consumer, Postgres outbox, materialized segments, and per-tenant Gateway admin eraser keep cost O(active tenants), not O(channels x contacts).
